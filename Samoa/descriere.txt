qualify.py is an autonomous tournament agent and chart recovery solver for Island Duel. It interacts with the native game engine to play a 512-match tournament across 32 imaginary island arenas, qualifies against the Apia coding festival champion bot, unlocks the homeward voyage star chart, and decodes the embedded cipher into the final flag. 

At every game turn, the agent evaluates all affordable actions and decides whether to claim a neutral tile, capture an enemy tile, or rest to restore energy. The decision logic balances immediate point gain, board exploration, and tactical energy conservation across four main principles:

1. Energy reserve management
Energy is capped at 9 and resting restores 3. When energy is at or below 4, resting delivers the maximum +3 benefit. The agent avoids spending energy down to 0 on low-value tiles, preventing forced rests where it is vulnerable. It holds an energy buffer so it can always afford high-weight claims (costing 3 to 4) and enemy captures (costing tile cost + 2, up to 6 energy). When energy is low, it rests unless an exceptional capture or a tile with weight 7 or more is available.
2. Territory swing and capture efficiency
Claiming a neutral tile increases the participant's score by that tile's weight (1 to 9). Capturing an adjacent enemy tile transfers the tile, increasing the participant's score while reducing the enemy's score, creating a 2x net margin swing. The agent calculates the point-to-cost ratio (swing divided by energy cost) to prioritize efficient gains over expensive low-value tiles.
3. Fog-of-war frontier expansion
At the start of each match, most tiles lie hidden under the fog of war. Claiming a tile reveals all its orthogonal neighbors. In early and mid-game (turns 1 to 90), the agent awards a priority bonus to tiles that have adjacent hidden neighbors, uncovering valuable ground and opening multiple routes toward the center of the board. In the late game (final 35 actions), exploration bonus drops so the agent focuses purely on locking in score margin.
4. Center control and counter-threat evaluation
Start tiles are fixed at opposite corners (0 and 224). The agent applies a positional bonus for tiles closer to the center (7, 7) to meet the opponent in the middle and deny territory. When considering tiles adjacent to existing enemy units, it assesses whether the opponent has enough energy to immediately recapture the tile on the next turn, avoiding costly ping-pong trades on contested borders.
5. Voyage star chart decryption
Upon completing the tournament, the engine unlocks the homeward star chart. The chart encodes 17 letter-bearing stars (5 guide stars representing the Southern Cross / Crux on the flag of Samoa, and 12 radial companion stars). The solver sorts the guide stars in Greek watch order (Alpha, Beta, Gamma, Delta, Epsilon Crucis), appends their nearer companion stars before the farther ones (at radii 18, 23, and 28), and decrypts the resulting 17-character ciphertext with the Vigenere key FAAVAEILEATUASAMOA (Samoa's national motto and foundation: "Fa'avae i le Atua Samoa").
